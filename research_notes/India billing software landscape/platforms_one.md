# BUSY, myBillBook, and Vyapar: Public Product Capabilities


## BUSY

### Takeaway
BUSY presents as an accounting-led desktop/cloud business system with GST, inventory, voucher workflows and increasingly connected mobile access. Its public feature map spans simple invoicing through multi-branch controls and production, with important capabilities tiered by edition; it appears aimed at businesses that need accounting depth and configurable operational workflows, rather than only fast invoice entry.

### Cited Findings
- BUSY markets one system for invoicing, accounting, GST, e-invoicing, e-way bills and inventory. Its homepage describes GST ledgers, ITC records and calculations, auto e-invoice with IRP integration/IRN/QR code, e-way bills, GSTR reconciliation, and mobile access to sales, stock and financial reports. [BUSY home](https://busy.in/)
- The inventory feature listing includes multi-GST rates within one bill, tax-inclusive/exclusive bills, TDS/TCS, e-way bills/e-invoices, WhatsApp/email/SMS invoice sharing, customizable print formats, barcode print/scan, party/date/quantity-specific pricing, pricing from purchase/last-sale price, stock warnings, past sales lookup, quotations, orders, challans and proforma invoices, and cash/UPI receipts. [BUSY inventory management](https://busy.in/accounting-software/inventory-management/)
- BUSY describes stock controls such as batch, expiry, serial number, parameters and custom attributes; multi-godown live stock and consolidated views are stated. [BUSY inventory management](https://busy.in/accounting-software/inventory-management/); [BUSY features and FAQs](https://busy.in/busy-features-and-faqs/)
- The official help FAQ explains configurable item master/voucher flows, with inventory vouchers recording batch-, parameter- or serial-wise data and inventory reports available from the display menu. It also lists multiple inventory valuation methods including FIFO, LIFO, last purchase, weighted average, average-price variants and self-evaluation. [BUSY inventory FAQ](https://busy.in/faqs/features/inventory-other-features/32/)
- A BUSY help page describes three editions: Basic includes GST invoices, accounting, inventory, mobile app and audit trail; Standard adds sales/purchase quotations, orders and challans, automatic e-way bills/e-invoices, retail POS, BOM/production and serial/parameter/batch stock; Enterprise adds voucher/master approval, indent management, enquiry/support management, multi-branches and HO/BO synchronization. Confirm feature entitlements against current edition/pricing before planning. [BUSY edition FAQ](https://busy.in/faqs/miscellaneous/suitable-disposition/1/)
- BUSY positions itself as desktop and online/cloud software and advertises a 15-day trial. Its public homepage says pricing depends on edition; the pricing page currently gives BUSY Online Access and SQL cloud charges, and explains mobile app inclusion/renewal and add-on charges. Price and offer details are volatile and should be checked at purchase. [BUSY home](https://busy.in/); [BUSY pricing](https://busy.in/pricing/)
- Sales and accounting are connected: the homepage describes vouchers, ledgers, GST and reports in one accounting system; feature pages say invoices sync with accounts and inventory, and business features cover orders, payments, receivables/payables, reports and branch operations. [BUSY home](https://busy.in/); [BUSY inventory management](https://busy.in/accounting-software/inventory-management/)
- The vendor publicly describes GSTR reconciliation across GSTR-1/2A/2B/3B with ITC mismatch tracking, e-invoice/IRN/QR output, and e-way bill generation linked to invoices. This supports compliance preparation and transaction-linked workflows; the source does not establish that BUSY itself submits all GST returns automatically. [BUSY home](https://busy.in/)

### Inferences
- Product master + voucher/accounting architecture appears central: day-to-day sales/purchase transactions likely update financial ledgers and stock together. This is inferred from the vendor’s explicit statements that billing syncs to accounts/inventory and that the product is organized around vouchers and ledgers. [BUSY home](https://busy.in/); [BUSY inventory management](https://busy.in/accounting-software/inventory-management/)
- BUSY’s edition segmentation suggests a path from a single-business accounting tool toward multi-location controls, approval workflows, manufacturing and deeper inventory traceability as businesses grow. [BUSY edition FAQ](https://busy.in/faqs/miscellaneous/suitable-disposition/1/)

### Gaps
- Public materials do not provide a full data model, API documentation, supported third-party integrations list, technical hosting/backup/security architecture, or full role-permission matrix. Do not assume those from feature marketing.
- Exact current edition-to-feature mapping can differ between the FAQ and current plans; confirm current offer, additional-user, multi-company, implementation and customization charges with BUSY. [BUSY pricing](https://busy.in/pricing/)
- The public GST claims describe reports, reconciliation and generation workflows, but not all return filing/approval steps or compliance responsibility boundaries.

## myBillBook

### Takeaway
myBillBook is positioned as a small-business-first web/mobile billing and inventory product: item/party records feed GST or non-GST invoices, purchases and stock, with payment collection, reports and accountant handoff layered on. Its public material includes more explicit workflow help than most, while features such as e-way/e-invoice, godowns, barcode, multi-user and business limits are plan-dependent and need confirmation.

### Cited Findings
- Its homepage describes GST and non-GST invoices, auto-tax, HSN/SAC, quotations, purchase orders and POS billing; inventory with low-stock alerts, batches, expiry and live updates; WhatsApp/SMS reminders and UPI links; reports/dashboard including cash flow, sales, dues, GSTR and Tally export; and GST reports, ITC, e-invoicing and e-way billing. [myBillBook home](https://mybillbook.in/)
- An official invoice help article describes a sales flow: select/add a party, add products/services with quantity, rate and HSN if applicable, optionally add charges/discount/tax details, generate, and share by WhatsApp/email or PDF. It says GST details are entered in invoice details and item records should carry HSN and GST rates. [Create a sales invoice](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/articles/4281361-how-to-create-a-sales-invoice-on-mybillbook)
- Item records can be bulk-imported from Excel and include prices, HSN/SAC, GST rates and stock quantities. Official FAQs describe categories, barcode generation/printing, PC scanner use during invoicing, and item imports from Excel, batch Excel, Vyapar and other software. [myBillBook FAQs](https://mybillbook.in/s/faqs/)
- Inventory features include automatic stock update from sales, purchases, returns and adjustments; low-stock/reorder alerts; barcode generation/scan; IMEI/serial tracking; batch/expiry; and reports. [Inventory management app](https://mybillbook.in/s/inventory-management-app/)
- Godown flow supports creating locations, assigning item quantities, selecting a godown on sales/purchase/return/note vouchers, transfers, location-level adjustments and availability. [Godown management](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/godown-management/)
- The vendor’s GST Reports page lists Balance Sheet, GSTR-1 (sales), GSTR-2 (purchase), GSTR-3B, GST sales/purchase with HSN, HSN-wise sales summary, TDS and TCS. It explicitly states reports depend on recorded invoices, purchases, tax rates and HSNs, and that reports do not file returns automatically; filing remains separate. [GST Reports](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/gst-reports/)
- e-way bill workflow starts from an existing sales invoice, reuses invoice details, requires setup/GSP credentials and transport details (distance, transporter, mode, vehicle); its help page states government rules apply and cancellation is subject to the time window. [e-Way Bill feature](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/eway-bill/). A recent help page also documents e-invoice/e-way bill workflows for sales returns, delivery challans and credit notes. [e-Way Bill/e-Invoice help](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/en/help/articles/9141249-how-to-use-the-new-e-way-bill-and-e)
- Reports include audit trail, bill-wise profit, cash/bank payments, staff-wise daybook/sales, expense reporting, P&L, purchase summary, stock detail/summary, godown-wise stock, low stock, batches, serial/IMEI, rate list, receivable ageing, party ledger and party-wise outstanding; filters and CA report sharing are described. GST reports are a separate report section. [Business reports](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/business-reports/)
- Online payment feature integrates with PhonePe Business: invoices can carry a PhonePe QR or Pay Now link; successful payments can automatically create a linked Payment-In entry. [Online payments](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/online-payments/)
- myBillBook publicly lists Tally export, online store, shared ledger, payment reminders, godown management, GST reports and staff attendance/payroll in its feature directory. [Feature directory](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/)
- Pricing pages surfaced during research show paid tiers, Android plan and plan-dependent device/business/user limits, GSTR exports, godown, POS, barcode, e-invoice/e-way bill and payroll features. Pages differ in age and plan/price details, so treat prices as snapshots and verify current offers. [Older detailed pricing page](https://freetrial.mybillbook.in/best-billing-software-v3.html); [current feature directory](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/)

### Inferences
- The product appears to favor low-friction, app-led setup: users can create parties/items in context, scan purchase bills, and reuse transaction records in stock and tax reports. That inference follows from the documented invoice, bill-scan and inventory flows. [Create a sales invoice](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/articles/4281361-how-to-create-a-sales-invoice-on-mybillbook); [Scan and Record Bills](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/scan-and-record-bills/)
- The documented reports organize business activity, but the vendor explicitly distinguishes report preparation from return filing, implying an accountant-review or external filing step for GST. [GST Reports](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/gst-reports/)
- The PhonePe collection flow suggests payments are not merely invoice annotations: successful online collections can feed payment bookkeeping automatically. [Online payments](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/online-payments/)

### Gaps
- No complete public role/permission matrix, API documentation, full list of payment rails/provider fees, or detailed security/backup architecture was found in the reviewed pages.
- Current plan entitlements and prices should be checked with vendor sales/product UI; an older detailed pricing landing page may no longer match the present catalogue.
- Product pages do not establish return filing submission to GSTN or validate end-to-end compliance outcomes; the GST reports page expressly says the reports do not file returns.

## Vyapar

### Takeaway
Vyapar presents as an owner-operated billing, inventory and bookkeeping application for small Indian businesses, with desktop/mobile options, offline use claims, auto stock updates, invoicing, orders, reminders and GST reports. Its pricing pages communicate distinct free, paid, POS/barcode and higher-tier manufacturing capabilities, though the official site currently has conflicting price/plan presentations across landing pages.

### Cited Findings
- Its pricing/product page lists professional invoices and quotations, purchase/sales orders, stock inventory, income/expenses/cash flow, payment reminders, reports and Excel item import. Invoice options include templates, signature, UPI QR, terms, regular/thermal printing and PDF sharing. [Vyapar pricing and features](https://vyaparapp.in/pricing-detail)
- The same page describes stock auto-updates on purchases and sales, categories, live stock, low-stock alerts and batch/expiry tracking; reports include P&L, purchase/sales orders, expenses, receivables/payables, daybook and cash flow; GST workflow includes invoice formats, bill-wise payments, due dates/overdues, and GSTR-1/2/3B/9 reports. [Vyapar pricing and features](https://vyaparapp.in/pricing-detail)
- Inventory pages describe vendor/purchase tracking, multiple suppliers, purchase order states, vendor-wise pricing and transaction history, GST-linked reports and FIFO stock valuation. [Vyapar inventory management](https://vyaparapp.in/free/erp-software/inventory); [Vyapar inventory software](https://vyaparapp.in/free/inventory-management-software)
- Official product pages claim barcode scanning, barcode label generation/printing, thermal printing and POS; a plan comparison places barcode, POS, barcode item import, stock transfer reports and weighing-machine integration in higher tiers. [Vyapar inventory management](https://vyaparapp.in/free/erp-software/inventory); [inventory pricing comparison](https://billing.vyaparapp.in/free/inventory-management-software)
- A detailed order-management page describes creating and converting sales/purchase orders, delivery challans, order statuses (open/partially delivered/closed), stock reservation at sales-order creation and stock deduction on invoice/challan conversion, advance/partial payments, WhatsApp order sharing and multi-device sync. [Vyapar order management](https://vyaparapp.in/free/inventory-management-software/order-management)
- An official business page claims GST/non-GST invoices with HSN/SAC, transaction tax rates, GSTR-1/GSTR-3B and e-way bills, plus online/offline usage and automatic sync after reconnecting. The offline/sync detail is a vendor claim, not an independent technical verification. [Vyapar billing page](https://vyaparapp.in/city/free-billing-software-coimbatore)
- The pricing page surfaced a free 15-day trial and a ₹242/month annual-billing offer (GST treatment specified there), while an inventory comparison page advertises paid desktop tiers at different monthly equivalents, a free mobile tier and free desktop tiers. These are inconsistent presentation snapshots and should not be used as a definitive quote. [Vyapar pricing and features](https://vyaparapp.in/pricing-detail); [inventory pricing comparison](https://billing.vyaparapp.in/free/inventory-management-software)
- Plan feature comparisons list multi-device sync, multiple item prices, additional item fields, bulk item update, barcode/POS, transfers and manufacturing inventory/reporting at different levels. [inventory pricing comparison](https://billing.vyaparapp.in/free/inventory-management-software)

### Inferences
- Vyapar’s design appears to center on day-to-day proprietor workflows: capture bill/order, update stock, track dues and inspect simplified cash/profit/GST reports. That is an inference based on its feature and pricing pages, which foreground these tasks over advanced accounting configuration. [Vyapar pricing and features](https://vyaparapp.in/pricing-detail)
- The described reservation behavior implies that operational stock availability can distinguish committed sales-order quantity from stock physically deducted after fulfillment documents. [Vyapar order management](https://vyaparapp.in/free/inventory-management-software/order-management)
- The tier structure suggests monetization through device sync, POS/barcodes, multi-businesses, manufacturing and reporting/support upgrades, but confirm current mapping as offers differ across pages. [inventory pricing comparison](https://billing.vyaparapp.in/free/inventory-management-software)

### Gaps
- The reviewed public pages do not establish API/third-party accounting integrations, complete user permissions, GST return filing submission, payment-gateway fees, or detailed technical security/data residency architecture.
- The vendor’s public pricing pages conflict (different amounts, tiers and device/business limits). Treat exact prices, taxes, trial conditions and included features as unverified until checked in the current checkout or confirmed with sales.
- Some detailed workflows are on industry/SEO landing pages rather than a centralized help manual; confirm availability in the specific desktop/mobile plan before using them as product requirements.
