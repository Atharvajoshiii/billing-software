# India GST compliance and product implications

Research checked 17 November 2026. This is a product requirements scan, not legal advice. Treat statutory provisions and notifications as authority; GST Council recommendations are not operative law until enacted/notified. GST portal interfaces, validations, rate schedules and thresholds change and need a maintained source-of-truth process.

## What a GST-ready document and tax engine must model

### Takeaway
GST cannot safely be represented as a product's static percentage alone. A compliant document depends on seller registration, document and supply type, effective-dated item classification/rate, buyer and delivery facts, place-of-supply rules, discounts, and lifecycle state. Ledgerwise's current fixed GST-rate dropdown and invoice shape are an early billing feature, not a GST document engine.

### Cited Findings
- CGST Rule 46 specifies invoice data including supplier identity/GSTIN; a serial unique within a financial year; issue date; recipient details as applicable; HSN/SAC; description; goods quantity/UQC; total and taxable values after discounts; tax rates and amounts by tax component; inter-State place of supply/state; separate delivery address where relevant; reverse-charge flag; and supplier signature/digital signature. [CBIC, Rule 46](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html); [CBIC amended CGST Rules PDF, Rule 46](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/amended-01012022-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A.pdf)
- Other statutory/business documents include bills of supply, receipt/refund/payment vouchers, credit/debit notes, delivery challans, and revised invoices in applicable situations. CGST Act section 34 allows credit notes for overcharged value/tax, returned goods or deficient supplies and governs reporting/tax adjustment; credit/debit note data is part of GST returns. [CBIC invoice rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html); [CGST Act, Chapter VII](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/CGST-bill-e.html)
- A composition taxpayer issues a bill of supply, does not collect GST separately, and the document must indicate composition-taxpayer status and ineligibility to collect tax; exempt supplies also have a distinct document treatment. [CBIC composition rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/composition-rules.html); [CBIC sectoral FAQs](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/sectoral-faq.html)
- The IGST Act generally determines goods place of supply by where movement terminates for delivery, and includes a deemed rule for bill-to/ship-to. Service place-of-supply rules vary, with registered-recipient location as a general rule subject to exceptions. Supplier location compared with place of supply determines intra-State versus inter-State treatment; supplies to/by SEZ are inter-State. [IGST Act, sections 7–12](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/IGST-bill-e.html)
- CBIC's rates schedules are classification-specific and contain conditions and cess. The 56th GST Council recommendations were implemented for most goods and services from 22 September 2025 through notifications; the official FAQs distinguish specified tobacco products whose timing differed. This makes a hard-coded four/five-slab dropdown unreliable. [CBIC GST rates](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/gst-goods-services-rates.html); [56th GST Council recommendations](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163555&lang=2&reg=3); [official FAQs on implementation timing](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163560&lang=1&reg=6)
- GST Portal GSTR-1 guidance shows reportable categories include B2B, reverse charge, exports, B2C summaries, notes, advances and adjustments, HSN summaries, document series and e-commerce/section 9(5) supplies. From May 2025, HSN summary is split into B2B and B2C tabs; the portal also describes a ₹1 lakh inter-State B2CL threshold from August 2024. [GST Portal, GSTR-1 guide](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/Creation_of_Outward_Supplies_Return_in_GSTR-1.htm)

### Inferences
- Replace the current `gst_rate` as sole tax source with effective-dated tax classification records: HSN/SAC, supply category (taxable, nil, exempt, non-GST, zero-rated), rate and cess, notification/rate source, effective dates, UQC, and review state. Never infer classification purely from a product name; allow a seller/accountant to set it and retain the applicable classification on each issued document.
- Represent business GST registrations separately from the workspace: GSTIN, legal/trade name, State/UT, registration type/status/effective dates, seller address, financial year, invoice series and return cadence. Keep branch/location, warehouse/dispatch-from, bill-to, ship-to and place-of-supply as distinct facts.
- Build a rules-based tax calculation service with explainable components and a review/warning path for uncertain supply facts. Use the invoice/issue date and stored rule version to reproduce historical values. Store an immutable issued-document snapshot and correct through linked notes/cancellations/amendments instead of changing old invoices silently.
- Treat document number series as per-registration/per-document/per-financial-year configurable controls. Include exports/SEZ, reverse charge, advances, composition, mixed taxable/exempt supplies, tax-inclusive pricing, discounts, cess and credit/debit notes as explicit data dimensions rather than free text.
- The live GST Portal's post-2025 return tables and taxonomy should inform Ledgerwise's reporting adapter; do not assume the existing five-rate summary maps to filing-ready output.

### Gaps
- Rules for place of supply, time of supply, classification/rates, exemptions and credit adjustments are nuanced and notification-dependent. This research does not resolve treatment for a particular SKU/service or cover every sector/document exception; production rules need legal/accountant review and current Acts/rules/notifications.
- Exact HSN/SAC digits and other reporting validations depend on law, turnover and transaction context. Avoid a universal HSN length rule without checking current notification and portal validation for the taxpayer's case.

## Registration, returns, and input-tax-credit workflow

### Takeaway
Full GST management requires a controlled preparation and reconciliation workflow across GSTR-1/IFF, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B/IMS, tax payments, and adjustments. A sales-only tax dashboard is far short of return preparation or filing; Ledgerwise currently labels its summaries a preview and has no portal integration.

### Cited Findings
- Eligible taxpayers under the GST Portal's QRMP scheme file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B quarterly while paying tax monthly; eligibility is PAN-based aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore subject to portal conditions and filing status. IFF is an optional facility for reporting B2B invoices/notes in the first two months of a quarter, with the cited portal FAQ giving the 13th of the next month as due date. [GST Portal QRMP/IFF FAQs](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/FAQs_change_profile.htm)
- GSTR-1 reporting includes categorized supply and document details; the Portal requires cross-checking summaries and validations, with Table 12 HSN split B2B/B2C from May 2025 and document issued reporting. E-invoice records populate specified GSTR-1 tables, but this does not itself establish that a full return is correct or filed. [GST Portal GSTR-1 guide](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/Creation_of_Outward_Supplies_Return_in_GSTR-1.htm); [GSTN e-invoice auto-population advisory](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/einovice_to_gstr1.pdf)
- GSTR-3B covers outward and reverse-charge tax, inter-State supplies, eligible ITC and reversals, exempt/nil/non-GST inward supplies, interest/late fee, and payment. The GST Portal displays system-computed values from GSTR-1/1A and GSTR-2B, permits some edits, and warns on differences. [GST Portal GSTR-3B guide](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/Create_and_Submit_GSTR3B.htm)
- GSTR-2B is a static, auto-drafted ITC statement derived from supplier GSTR-1/1A/IFF, GSTR-5, GSTR-6, and import data; it is not itself filed. Portal guidance says it supports ITC entries in GSTR-3B and explicitly cautions that the system does not identify every legally unavailable-credit case, so taxpayers must self-assess. [GST Portal GSTR-2B FAQ](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/FAQ_gstr2b.htm)
- GSTN's Invoice Management System (IMS) lets recipients accept, reject, or keep supplier invoice/record items pending for ITC handling; records then affect the GSTR-2B workflow. The official FAQ describes the system's interaction with return periods and GSTR-3B filing. [GSTN IMS FAQ](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/final_faqs_on_ims_22_09_2024.pdf); [GSTN revised IMS advisory](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/revised_advisory_on_ims.pdf)
- On 8 October 2026, the 57th GST Council made recommendations for further return and ITC process reforms, including additional GSTR-1/1A/IFF vs GSTR-3B reconciliation, RCM and ITC electronic statements, and IMS changes; the stated target for some return changes was April 2027. These are recommendations/timelines, not proof of current enacted requirements. [PIB, 57th GST Council recommendations](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2320934&lang=1&reg=3)

### Inferences
- Implement return periods and compliance state per GSTIN (monthly/QRMP as configured), with a preparer/reviewer workflow: period close → books reconciliation → exception queue → table-mapped preview/export → explicit approval → submit or portal handoff → confirmation/ARN/status capture. Keep portal-reported, Ledgerwise-computed and user-adjusted values side by side with a reason and timestamp.
- Add purchase-side capture and GSTIN/vendor invoice matching, import of GSTR-2B data, IMS action tracking, eligible/ineligible/reversal categories, RCM, ITC evidence and reconciliation. Surface mismatch categories (missing from books, missing from portal, tax/period/GSTIN mismatch, duplicate, amendment, likely ineligible) without representing an automated match as a legal entitlement decision.
- Make accounting ledger and tax return reconciliation separate but linked: invoices/purchases/notes/receipts/payments feed books; tax liability and cash/credit ledger allocation feed a return reconciliation view. Track return due status, acknowledgements, late fees/interest where supported, and final filed snapshots.
- As of Nov 2026, design the compliance model to absorb the Council's proposed 2027 changes, but do not hard-code them as law until notified and portal forms/implementation are live.

### Gaps
- This research did not confirm exact live GSTN API access rights, authorization/certification requirements, official direct-filing pathway for a third-party SaaS, current due-date calendars for all taxpayer types, or all IMS/ITC conditions. Product planning should separately confirm legal authority, GST Suvidha Provider/ASP/IRP arrangements, portal terms, and integration documentation.
- The 57th Council items are recommendations and some include target dates; the Gazette/CBIC notifications and GST Portal release behavior must be rechecked before implementation.

## E-invoice and e-way bill integrations

### Takeaway
E-invoicing and e-way bills are separate government-system workflows with eligibility checks, strict schema and timing validations, acknowledgements, QR/IRN data, transport details, failure states and cancellation/replacement procedures. The correct architecture is a versioned compliance integration with an audit trail; a printable PDF alone does not complete the mandated IRP workflow.

### Cited Findings
- IRP's mandate page states the current threshold is AATO ₹5 crore or more in any financial year from FY 2017–18 onward, with exclusions by taxpayer/entity type; the page specifies regular taxpayers and SEZ developers as able to generate e-invoices. The threshold is PAN-wide in the mandate materials and historical turnover matters, not only current-year turnover. [IRP e-invoice mandate](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/einvoice-mandate/)
- IRP validates the prescribed invoice data, returns a unique IRN and signed QR/invoice payload, and passes data into GST systems for GSTR-1 population. The GST Portal says those records can take up to two days to appear and users can download/review the details. [IRP FAQ](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/faq-powered-by-irisirp/); [GST Portal e-invoice/GSTR-1 guide](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/Creation_of_Outward_Supplies_Return_in_GSTR-1.htm)
- Since 1 April 2025, taxpayers with AATO ₹10 crore or more must report invoices, debit notes and credit notes to IRP within 30 days of document date; IRP rejects submissions beyond the window. [IRP advisory](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/revised-time-limit-for-e-invoice-reporting-for-businesses-with-aato-of-%E2%82%B910-crores-above/)
- The e-way bill portal describes the general requirement for movement of goods where consignment value exceeds ₹50,000, for supply and some non-supply/inward movements, subject to rule/notification exemptions. Portal FAQ says wrong submitted data cannot be edited; cancel and generate a correct replacement. EWB generation needs invoice/bill/challan and transport details; movement requirements vary by exemption and State/UT. [NIC E-Way Bill portal](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/); [official EWB FAQ](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/html/ewb_qna.html)
- The official EWB API release notes describe validations such as generation within 180 days of the document date and restriction on extension beyond 360 days, plus active GSTIN, HSN/document and duplicate checks. The same release notes dated 30 July 2026 say the previously announced Ship-to GSTIN and voluntary EWB closure changes were put on hold; the May 2026 update was withdrawn. Do not build withdrawn API changes as live law. [NIC EWB API release notes](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/release-notes.html)
- A GSTN advisory dated 17 June 2026 specified proposed API changes for Ship-to GSTIN and voluntary EWB closure, including conditional mandatory fields and a planned 1 August production rollout; the official NIC EWB release notes dated 30 July subsequently said the recent changes were on hold. This is a direct example of why checking advisories and release notes immediately before deployment matters. [GSTN June 2026 advisory PDF](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/advisory_einvoice_api_ewb_by_irn_approved.pdf); [NIC EWB API release notes](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/release-notes.html)
- GST Council recommendations from October 2026 include extending e-invoicing to domestic purchases from unregistered suppliers under RCM and import of services for taxpayers with AATO ₹5 crore+, but this is a recommendation, not evidence it is currently mandated. [PIB, 57th GST Council recommendations](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2320934&lang=1&reg=3)

### Inferences
- Build an integration boundary with multiple gateway/IRP credentials, per-GSTIN eligibility profile (historical PAN AATO, taxpayer type/exclusion, source/date), idempotency, retries, request/response retention, failure recovery, duplicate prevention and a state machine such as draft → locally issued → IRN pending → registered/rejected → cancelled/replaced. Keep external acknowledgement and signed QR/IRN attached to the issued snapshot.
- For e-way bill, model movement/consignment independently from the invoice: dispatch-from, bill-to, ship-to, PIN/state, value, HSN, movement reason, transporter, mode, vehicle/trans-document, distance, validity and cancel/reissue links. Include state-specific rule configuration and configurable fields so schema changes do not require rewriting historical transactions.
- Place hard compliance gates on eligibility and current external registration state; never label a covered unregistered local PDF “compliant.” Provide an offline/pending state and surface a clear operational path for retry/assisted completion.
- Maintain a compliance release calendar and regression fixture set based on official schema/advisory versions. Save the source notification/rule/API version used for each affected transaction and integration submission.

### Gaps
- Current authorized IRP/GSP list, onboarding process and account-level credential policies, private IRP pricing/limits, sandbox/production access agreement, retry/rate limits, service-level commitments and outage contingency were not established by the official public pages reviewed. These require an integration/vendor/legal review.
- State/UT-specific intrastate EWB thresholds and exemptions, commodity-based exceptions, current schemas and credentials need live verification. The NIC pages show evolving validations and advisories, and one 2026 enhancement sequence was rolled back/held.
